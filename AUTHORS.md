# Authors and attribution

Yao Xu is the author and maintainer of this formalization project.

The positive half follows the construction of Pardo-Simón and Sixsmith, together with Rempe-Gillen's approximation work; this formalization proves the approximation step for the tracts used in the construction. The negative half follows Li's argument for a finite bound along a cofinal set of radii. The development formalizes these arguments and does not claim a new mathematical solution. Its positive proof builds the tract and conformal-map estimates before deriving unbounded maximum-point counts. Its negative proof obtains arbitrarily large radii at which the count has a fixed finite upper bound.

The author planned, directed and reviewed the work, using AI coding assistants; all proofs and their dependencies are checked by the Lean kernel.

The project uses Mathlib and the vendored libraries listed in [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).
